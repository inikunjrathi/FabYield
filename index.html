Cost per good chip

Inputs

One month of production. The figures supplied are illustrative, not from a real fab. Change any of them and every account below recalculates.

Process accounts

Normal loss is worth only its scrap value, so good units carry its cost. Abnormal loss and gain are valued like good units and leave the process account for profit and loss. There is no opening or closing work in progress.

How the cost builds up

Process cost absorbs normal loss only. The all-in figure adds the net abnormal loss, which shows what each good chip cost the business but is not used to value stock.

Standard against actual yield

Variances compare actual cost with the standard allowed for the good chips produced. F is favourable and A is adverse.

Impact on profit and loss

The same month under two methods. Process costing charges the yield shortfall as abnormal loss. Standard costing charges it through variances. Charging both in one statement would count the same lost wafers twice.
